Privacy Policy

GangaClips Publisher · Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Who we are

GangaClips Publisher ("we", "the app") is a tool operated by GangaClips (Colombia) to publish short video clips to TikTok and YouTube accounts that their owners connect. Contact: gangaclips.col@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • TikTok: when you connect your account we receive your basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) and an access token that allows us to upload and publish videos you choose (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • YouTube (Google): an access token for the scope youtube.upload, used only to upload the videos you choose to your channel.
  • The videos, titles and descriptions that are published through the app.

We do not collect passwords, payment data, contacts, messages, location or any data from other users.

3. How we use it

Only to publish the clips you authorize to your own account and to show you the result (link to the published video). We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok or Google when publishing, as required to provide the service.

5. Google API Services

GangaClips Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted (as secrets) and are never published in code or logs. Videos are processed on temporary servers and deleted after publishing.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep tokens only while your account is connected. You can revoke access at any time: TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Google → myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete all data we hold about you, write to gangaclips.col@gmail.com and we will delete it within 7 days.

8. Children

The app is not directed to anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and its date.
