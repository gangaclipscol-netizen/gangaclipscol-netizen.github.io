GangaClips Publisher

Publish short vertical clips to your own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What it does

  1. You sign in with TikTok or Google (YouTube) and authorize GangaClips Publisher.
  2. Our tool prepares short vertical clips (9:16) with subtitles and credit to the original creator.
  3. You review the title and description, and the clip is published to your own account.

We only publish to the accounts you connect. You can disconnect at any time from your TikTok or Google account settings.

Who uses it

Clip channel creators, starting with the GangaClips team (Colombia), who post highlights from live streams with credit to each streamer.
